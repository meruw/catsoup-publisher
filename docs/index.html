Working with TikTok

From a local draft to your TikTok inbox.

  1. Connect each account

    In Accounts, choose ES or EN, then sign in to TikTok. Check the account TikTok returns and repeat for the other language.

  2. Send the approved cut

    Open the final video’s publication draft and confirm the destination. When your app permissions allow it, Publisher transfers the video to your TikTok inbox. Open the notification to edit and finish posting in TikTok.

  3. Manage your data

    Remove a connection in Publisher’s Accounts section. To revoke access, use the platform’s connected-app settings. The privacy policy explains local storage and deletion.
