CATsoup Publisher

CATsoup Publisher is a Windows desktop app for creators working in Spanish and English. It helps you check the final video, prepare a caption and send it to the account you choose for that language.

Connect a TikTok account

In Accounts, select ES or EN, choose TikTok, then connect your account in the TikTok sign-in window. Publisher displays the account TikTok returns. Repeat for the other language.

Send a video to your inbox

Choose the approved final video, open its publication draft and confirm the destination. Publisher transfers the video to TikTok. Open the notification in your TikTok inbox to edit and finish posting. Access depends on your TikTok app permissions.

Manage your data

Use Accounts to remove a connection from Publisher, and the connected-app settings on the platform to revoke its access. See the privacy policy for local storage and deletion instructions.
