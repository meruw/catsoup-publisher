These terms cover use of CATsoup Publisher to connect accounts, prepare drafts and send videos. The desktop application is maintained through the CATsoup Publisher project.

Connecting and uploading

Connect accounts you are authorized to manage. Use videos, music, text and other material that you have permission to share. Check the destination account, language and visibility before confirming a transfer. Each platform’s terms and content rules also apply.

For TikTok, Publisher can send the video to your inbox for completion in TikTok or use Direct Post with the privacy and disclosures you explicitly choose, subject to client approval and provider restrictions. Confirming publication can make a YouTube video, Instagram Reel, Facebook Page Reel or X post visible on the connected destination according to the selected mode. Provider permissions, limits and interruptions can affect delivery. If an upload’s result is unclear, check the destination before retrying.

Software license

The project distributes its original software under GPL-3.0-only. That license governs copying, modification, distribution and warranty for the software. See the project’s third-party notices.

Stopping use and support

You can remove accounts, revoke provider access and stop using Publisher at any time. For support, use project issues. Issues are public; keep passwords, tokens and private account details out of reports.
